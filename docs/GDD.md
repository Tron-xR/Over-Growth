# GDD
- Genre: puzzle adventure, Metroidvania
- Players: single-player, offline, no paywall
- Platform: PC, console
- Visuals: stylized hand-painted low-poly 3D characters/environments; 2D sprites for dialogue and interaction
- Camera: third-person, top-angled, fixed over the player
- Tone: calm exploration, post-apocalyptic, melancholic

## Core loop
Explore → find part/ability/log → solve puzzle gate → unlock area → repeat. Manage battery vs. part efficiency; respawn on depletion.

## Pillars
1. Exploration  2. Puzzle solving  3. Part-based customization

## Player
Non-combat. Moves on the ground plane: walk, run, dash. Energy (battery) drains with movement, scaled by equipped parts.

## Senses (progression layer)
Start: thinking only (dialogue thoughts). Regain: sight (intro room), hearing, smell. Each sense unlocks a presentation/gameplay layer (see SYSTEMS.md §Senses).

## Rewards
- Abilities (4): core transfer + 3 TBD, gated to far-apart areas
- Custom parts: speed, push/pull force, power consumption, slot cost
- Shiny collectibles: optional, post-game quest
- Logs: lore from NPCs/researchers

## Out of scope (v1)
Combat, multiplayer, online, monetization.

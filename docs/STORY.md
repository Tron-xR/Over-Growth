# Story
## Premise
A visionary scientist wanted to free humanity from fragile bodies by moving brains into mechanical ones. He died before decoding emotions; his brain was preserved. Centuries later the research "succeeded" without emotions: humanity became walking brains in suits, Earth became servers and power plants, and death stopped mattering — resources did.

## History
1. Gen-1 clankers hoard resources to live forever.
2. Later generations are superior but starve; divide grows.
3. New-gen groups revolt, demand sharing, are refused → great war → resources exhausted.
4. Survivors: hybrid cyborgs and humans who refused the change. Nature reclaims cities. The lab regains power; the brain wakes.

## Player
The creator, a brain in a jar, confronting the consequences of his invention. Voiced via on-screen thought dialogue.

## NPCs
- Old man (city): recharges battery when low; acts as city respawn. If player shuts down on the road: "found you on my evening walk lying on the road."
- Scavenger: cyborg deserter from the war (still has fear), scavenges robot corpse piles, rude, sells parts for the right cost.
- Cat: small robotic cat that stores large amounts of power (see concept art) — role TBD (battery pickup / puzzle).

## Opening
